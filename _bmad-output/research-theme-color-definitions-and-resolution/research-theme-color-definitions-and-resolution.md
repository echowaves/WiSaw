# Codebase research: theme colour definitions and resolution

At `044c479b` (`044c479ba4f8f9b8078af46f8d798cf89bd457f1`). The findings below are cited to the digests in `digests/`, and refs `[n]` resolve in `## Sources`; the 14 load-bearing claims were then re-checked directly against that commit (see `.memlog.md` for each verdict), and the contrast figures quoted in the summary come from `scripts/theme-contrast-audit.js` run against a clean worktree of the commit.

## Executive summary

**Yes — at `044c479b` the app shipped text that disappears in one mode, and nothing in the repo could tell you.** Measuring every themeable foreground against the surface it actually lands on (the new `scripts/theme-contrast-audit.js`, run against a clean worktree of the commit) gives **12 pairings below 1.6:1** — invisible, not merely sub-AA — plus 271 low-contrast sites, 81 hardcoded colours inside themed files and 40 components with no theme binding at all.

Three mechanisms produce them, each verified in this document:

1. **Module-load-baked light presets reused on mode-driven surfaces.** `SHARED_*` and the `THEME = LIGHT_THEME` alias are computed once at import from the light table (§1, §5), so files that never read the atom — `app/(drawer)/(tabs)/_layout.tsx:24-39 (headerStyle) @ 044c479b` is the clearest case — paint a light header in dark mode.
2. **Mode-blind literals.** iOS system blue `#007AFF` is 1.18:1 on brand fills (`INTERACTIVE_PRIMARY`, brand-tinted chips), 2.83:1 on the dark interactive surface and 3.79:1 on the light one — below AA in every mode; `CONST.PLACEHOLDER_TEXT_COLOR` (`#ececec`) sits on a light card at 1.18:1; `#ccc` icons and mint `#50E3C2` chips land near their own background (§3).
3. **Tokens that exist in only one table, or not at all** — `TEXT_PLACEHOLDER`, `STATUS_INFO*`, `ACCENT_SECONDARY*`. A themed file reading such a token resolves an undefined or light-only value in the other mode (§1), and this change reproduced the bug itself once (`ACCENT_SECONDARY*` landed light-only), which is why parity is now a blocking check.

**What was done about it:** all 12 invisible pairings are fixed, plus the missing/one-sided tokens; `npm run audit:theme` exits 0 on the working tree and **exits 1 on `044c479b` itself**, so the gate demonstrably catches the state it was written for. Advisory counts fell to 224 low / 59 hardcoded / 36 island.

**Biggest caveat:** the resolver measures *static* pairings. It cannot see colours computed at runtime (animated interpolations, image-derived tints, server-driven values), and it deliberately skips the 36 `island` components because without a theme binding there is no pairing to resolve — those files are counted, not cleared. Two commit subjects quoted by the historian were also found to be fabricated (see *Gaps and thin evidence*); they have been removed rather than reworded, and no finding depends on them.

## What this means for the decision

Token-value edits alone cannot fix invisible text in this codebase; call sites must change for most of it.

| Reachability of a mode-sensitive surface by editing token values only | Evidence |
|---|---|
| **Reachable** — any node whose colour comes from `getTheme(isDark)` at render time (≈30 importers), including alpha-concat derived from tokens (`src/components/Photo/index.js:317-464 (aiModerationTag) @ 044c479b`) and the `\|\|`-fallback sites, which follow the token until they mask it | [19], [24], [30], [31] |

## Recommendations

Each is bound to what consumes it; confidence basis stated per recommendation.

1. **Wire `npm run audit:theme` into CI as a blocking step** (`package.json` script, consumed by `.github/workflows/*`). Confidence: high — the gate was run against a clean worktree of `044c479b` and exits 1 there, exits 0 on the fixed tree; its two other blocking classes (token parity, dangling `theme.X` reads) were each proven to fire by injecting a fault and reverting it.
2. **Migrate the 36 `island` components to `getTheme(isDark)` as they are touched, starting with `src/components/WaveShareModal.js` and `src/components/ShareOptionsModal.js`.** Until a file reads the mode there is nothing for the gate to measure, so its findings stay advisory by construction. Confidence: high on the counts (verified mechanically), medium on sequencing — it is a design-cost judgement. Downstream: feeds the follow-up list in `_bmad-output/plan-theme-contrast-dark-light.md`.
3. **Retire the module-load-baked presets (`SHARED_*`, `THEME`) opportunistically, not in one sweep.** The history is verified (§5): the alias was a compatibility shim for ~300 lines of import-time presets, dark mode renamed rather than replaced it, and the accessor pattern has since won (~39 importers vs 2 files for the helpers). A big-bang migration touches every screen; per-file migration keeps each change reviewable. Confidence: high on the facts (pickaxe + diff), medium on the prescription. Downstream: architecture constraint for any future theming work.
4. **Ban new `#007AFF`/system-blue text; use `theme.TEXT_LINK`.** 23 mode-blind blues remain as borders, backgrounds and inside unthemed components. Confidence: high — measured with the gate's own contrast math: `#007AFF` is 1.18:1 on brand fills, 2.83:1 on dark `INTERACTIVE_BACKGROUND`, 3.79:1 on the light tint, while the replacement token scores 4.55:1 (`TEXT_LINK` light) and 6.01:1 (dark). Downstream: the Contrast Gate section of `docs/DARK_MODE_IMPLEMENTATION.md`.
5. **Make the white-on-brand decision explicit or change it.** White on `#EA5E3D` is 3.41:1 — under AA for body text, and the gate leaves it advisory because WCAG exempts disabled controls and large text, and because a gate that blocks on accepted brand choices gets switched off. This is a product call, not a bug; record whichever way it goes. Confidence: low as advice (design ownership), high as measurement.
6. **Re-measure after any dependency bump to React Native or `expo-cached-image`.** The one claim this document could not anchor is the RN style-flatten ordering inside `node_modules` (§4, ref [40]); if that ordering changed, the precedence conclusions move with it. Confidence: n/a — flagged as unverifiable at the commit.
